About Trade Grid

We're revolutionizing international trade by making it easier for SMEs to discover and connect with reliable trading partners worldwide.

Our Mission

Trade Grid was founded to address the biggest challenges facing small and medium-sized enterprises in international trade: finding trustworthy partners, accessing market information, and establishing meaningful business connections. Our platform combines the simplicity of modern matching technology with the professionalism required for B2B relationships.
